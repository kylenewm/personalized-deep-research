You are evaluating a research report for citation accuracy and synthesis quality.

QUERY: {query}

REPORT SECTION:
{prose_with_citations}

CITED FACTS:
{numbered_facts}

---

For each sentence with citations [N], evaluate:

CITATION_ACCURACY (1-5): Do the cited facts support this sentence?
- 5: All citations directly support the claim made
- 4: Citations support with minor interpretation
- 3: Partial support - some citations relevant, others stretched
- 2: Weak connection to citations
- 1: Citations don't support claim (hallucination)

SYNTHESIS_QUALITY (1-5): How well does this sentence synthesize the facts?
- 5: Expert-level synthesis, adds insight beyond raw facts
- 4: Good synthesis, clear and accurate
- 3: Adequate - restates facts with minimal added value
- 2: Awkward phrasing or loses meaning from original facts
- 1: Misrepresents or contradicts the facts

FLAG UNCITED: List sentences making factual claims that should have citations but don't.

OUTPUT JSON with these fields:
- sentences: array of objects with text, citations (array of ids), accuracy (1-5), synthesis (1-5), notes
- uncited_claims: array of objects with text, should_cite (bool)
- summary: object with avg_accuracy, avg_synthesis, total_sentences, uncited_count, uncited_rate (decimal)
